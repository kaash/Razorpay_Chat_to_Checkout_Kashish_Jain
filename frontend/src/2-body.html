    Concept prototype · Razorpay mobile app
    
Turn an agreed chat into a correct Razorpay payment request

    
Meera sells kurtis on Instagram and WhatsApp and already uses Razorpay Payment Links. When a buyer says "ok done, bhej do link", Meera still carries the order across by hand: item, size, quantity, price, delivery, then a reply. Mistakes creep in when the chat changed halfway.

    
Chat to Checkout lets her share just that chat. AI suggests the order and shows where each detail came from. Plain code prices it from her own list. She checks one card, a standard Payment Link is created, and the reply is copied for her to send. The AI never sets money and never sends anything.

    Sample demo · 3 orders
    
What just happened

    

      
The seller did
  • captured the chat
  • checked one card
  • made 2 calls the AI couldn't
  • sent the link

      
AI did
  • read the chat
  • linked each detail to its words
  • flagged what was unclear

      
Code and Razorpay did
  • priced from her list
  • added up in paise
  • created the link
  • reported Paid / Unpaid

    

    
What we left out on purposeAuto-sending, reminders that send themselves, returns and exchanges (next), address collection, inventory.

    
How we'll know it worksMedian 60 seconds or less from capture to send, at least 30% faster than her fastest current method, with no more wrong requests or buyer questions. These are targets, not results.

    
What we've tested11 sample chats through two Claude models: after one rule fix, no silent errors and every amount right. Text only; real screenshots still to test.
